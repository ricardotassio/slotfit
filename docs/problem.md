# Problem contract

## User

A synthetic scheduling coordinator reviewing alternatives after a cancellation.

## Trigger

One or more appointments are cancelled inside a partially scheduled day.

## Decision supported

Which factually eligible wait requests form the best plan under declared operational objectives?

## Baselines

- FIFO eligible.
- Local best-fit.

## Proposed approach

Eligibility rules followed by a day-horizon constraint optimizer. The coordinator remains the decision-maker.

## Success boundary

The controlled experiment measures schedule behavior only. It does not measure patient acceptance, revenue, no-show reduction or clinical benefit.
